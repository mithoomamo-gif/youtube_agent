Samundar ki gehrai mein aisi duniya chhupi hai jahan sooraj ki roshni kabhi nahi pohanchti. Wahan ajeeb rangon wali machhliyan apni roshni khud banati hain aur andheray mein chamakti rehti hain. Samundar ka sab se gehra hissa Mariana khaai hai jo lagbhag gyarah kilometer gehri hai. Agar Mount Everest ko wahan rakha jaye to us ki choti bhi paani se kai meter neechay reh jayegi. Samundar mein namak ki miqdar itni zyada hai ke agar saara namak zameen par bichha diya jaye to poori duniya par namak ki moti teh ban jayegi. Mahireen kehte hain ke insaan ne chand ki satah ka naqsha samundar ke farsh se zyada behtar banaya hai. Har saal nayi ajeeb makhlooqat wahan se daryaft hoti rehti hain aur science dan hairan reh jate hain ke zindagi kitni sakht halat mein bhi mumkin hai wahan.
