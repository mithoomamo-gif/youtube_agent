YouTube Agent

YouTube Agent is a Windows desktop application that helps a channel owner produce long, faceless YouTube videos end to end: topic research, script writing, voiceover, stock visuals, editing, thumbnails, and uploading to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How it works

  • The app runs entirely on the user's own computer — there is no YouTube Agent server.
  • The user signs in with their own Google account and explicitly authorises the app to access their YouTube channel through the official YouTube API Services.
  • Videos are uploaded with the user's approval, and the user can disconnect their channel and revoke access at any time.

Your data

Your Google user data is used only to operate your channel from your own PC, as described in our Privacy Policy. Your data is never sold, and no copy of it is sent to the developer.
